Notification Bot (Nov 17 2026 at 05:37):

In the web, desktop and mobile apps, you can now hide website and video previews in a message that you find distracting.

Web and desktop updates

  • Drafts of the message you're composing now autosave as you're typing.
  • Hovering over an emoji in a message or a user's status now shows an enlarged preview of the emoji, with its name.
  • You can now focus your search so results are scrolled to a specified date.
  • You can now easily navigate between related conversations by viewing the links to and from a conversation.

Mobile updates

  • Clicking on a notification now navigates to the specific message that triggered the notification.
  • Notifications for iOS users are now grouped by conversation.
  • Android users can now attach multiple photos and videos when composing a message.
  • You can now report problematic messages if moderation requests are enabled.

For channels where it’s important that everyone stays on top of new messages, such as a channel for urgent announcements, organization administrators can enable mobile notifications by default for new subscribers.
